How You Can Help

Youth Protection

Scouts BSA places the greatest importance on creating the most secure environment possible for our youth members. To maintain such an environment, Scouts BSA developed numerous procedural and leadership selection policies and provides parents and leaders with resources.

Troop Support

You are always welcome to join us on campouts to provide two-deep leadership. We also need drivers, mentors, and leaders. If you have a trailer hitch, you may be our new best friend!

Learn More

Merit Badge Counselors

Whatever your area of expertise or interest — a craft or hobby, a profession, or a life skill — as a merit badge counselor, you offer your time and knowledge so that Scouts can explore a topic of interest.
