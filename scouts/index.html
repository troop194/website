How Much Do You Want From Scouting?

If you want to be a Scout so that you can spend time with your friends, go camping, and do other fun things, welcome. We will enjoy your company, and having you along will make our activities more successful. Scouting will give you the opportunity to do numerous things that wouldn't normally be available to you this early in your life. You will learn many useful skills, and the values taught by Scouting can help make your life, and the lives of those around you better.

As you spend time with us, you will notice that those that put more into Scouting will seem to get the most out of it. There are things you can do beyond attending troop meetings and going on campouts that can help you get more out of the time you spend with us.

Service

A key concept of scouting is service to others. Some opportunities for this will be fundraising events and service projects. Everyone will appreciate your help, and you will gain from these experiences.

Qualified older Scouts may be nominated to join the Order of the Arrow, Scouting's national honor society.

Rank

If the only rank you ever earn is the Scout rank, we hope you become the best Scout the world has ever known. Earning higher ranks, however, will do much more for you than just gaining the respect of your fellow scouts.

With each rank, skills are learned that can be part of your growth as a person, and also give you the opportunity to do more things.

Lower ranks focus on scouting skills and prepare you to have fun on camping trips. These ranks are Scout, Tenderfoot, Second Class, and First Class.

The higher ranks focus on leadership skills, merit badges, and service. These ranks are Star, Life, and Eagle.

Merit Badges

A wide variety of interests and vocations are represented by over 135 merit badges. Merit Badge books provide good, well-written information about each subject, and also explain the requirements for earning each badge.

If the troop does not have the book(s) you are looking for, visit a local library, or buy your own copy at the Scout Shop downtown or online. The troop prides itself in giving scouts access to help with many merit badges and provides a list of its merit badge counselors.

Leadership

There are opportunities for all of our Scouts to do things that benefit Scouting, our troop, and the scouts themselves. Study our list of troop positions using the link below.

Elections and appointments for these positions are usually held twice each year. Be a Den Chief or the person that keeps track of the troop library. Scouts with more experience working with people can be patrol leaders and their assistants. They work to plan our activities.
